About this search

Assisted retrieval, not an epileptiform-discharge or seizure diagnosis. Current processing uses native-rate filter, reference and difference settings. Gain and positive affine scaling do not change normalized shape; the search does not use screenshot pixels or decimated envelopes.

Elastic morphology adds voltage/slope DTW evidence (±5% local alignment) without demoting strict-correlation matches. Nearby durations searches 0.8×, 1× and 1.2× reference length where valid, not every duration. 128-point refinement; bounded candidate pool, not exhaustive DTW. Closest candidates may all be below the score guide. A candidate is not a confirmed finding; an empty search cannot rule out abnormal activity.

Results keep their original processing recipe when you change the view. Open restores that recipe with at least 2 s of surrounding context on each side where available. Review shape and background, asymmetry, slow components and spatial field; scalp criteria must not be automatically applied to intracranial contacts.

Feedback is session-only until you export it. Export contains channel labels, time ranges and review notes; treat it as sensitive. No clinical events are automatically written.
